Privacy

What ro sends, what it doesn't, and what happens when you buy pro. Last updated 2026-11-22.

This website

No cookies, no analytics, and no requests to other sites while you browse. Fonts come from your own device. Checkout opens on Polar, which has its own privacy policy.

The ro app

  • Update checks. Once a day ro asks GitHub whether a new version exists, and installs it automatically. GitHub sees your IP address and the app version.
  • Your files stay on your Mac. Search and tidying run on your Mac. ro doesn't send file contents or lists of paths, and it doesn't use an AI service to search.
  • Getting your pro key. If you buy pro from ro’s menu (Get pro…), ro asks our license service for your key: every 15 seconds for up to 2 hours after you start checkout, then once a day while pro is on, to pick up renewals. It sends only a random install ID made on your Mac: no files, paths, name, or email. You can turn this off in ro → License…. Either way, the key itself is checked on your Mac.
  • AI handoff puts a note on your clipboard and opens the AI app you choose. ro doesn't send it. Secrets and personal details are hidden in the note.
  • Activity history is off by default. If you turn it on, it stays in ~/.ro on your Mac for 7 days.

When you buy pro

  • Polar, our merchant of record, collects your name, email, country, and payment details to charge you, handle tax, and send receipts. We never see your card details.
  • Polar tells our license service your email address and order details. The service signs your key and emails it through our email provider (Resend). If you bought from ro, it also keeps your key under a scrambled (hashed) form of ro’s install ID so ro can pick it up, until 30 days after the key expires. It keeps nothing else about you.
  • We use your email only for your key, renewals, and replies to messages you send us.
