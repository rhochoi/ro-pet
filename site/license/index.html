License help

Keys, renewals, and moving to a new Mac.

Where is my key?

It's in the email we sent right after payment, with "License key" in the subject. The key is one line starting with RO1. Check your spam folder if you can't find it.

Automatic key pickup

When you buy from ro (menu bar icon → Get pro…), ro picks up your key and each renewal by itself. It sends only a random install ID made on your Mac. To stop it, open ro → License… and uncheck Get my key automatically. Keys you paste work either way.

My subscription renewed

If you bought from ro, nothing to do. Otherwise each renewal sends a new key with a new expiry date. Paste it the same way: ro menu bar icon → License… → Save key. Until you do, your old key keeps working for 14 days past its period.

New Mac or reinstall

Open ro on the new Mac and paste the same key. You can use pro on all the Macs you personally use.

Check or remove the key

ro menu bar icon → License… shows the current status and expiry date. Remove key takes ro back to the free version on that Mac. Your files and history stay as they are.

"This version of ro doesn't know this key"

Update ro (menu bar icon → Check for updates…) and paste the key again.

Manage your subscription

Cancel, change plan, get invoices, or ask for a refund in Polar's customer portal. Or write to us: support address coming soon.
