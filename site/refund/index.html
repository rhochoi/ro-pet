Refunds

Last updated 2026-11-22.

14 days, no questions

If pro isn't for you, ask for a full refund within 14 days of a payment. That covers your first purchase and each renewal.

How to ask

Ask in Polar's customer portal, or write to our support address (coming soon) with the email you used to buy. Polar, our merchant of record, sends the refund to your original payment method.

Open the customer portal

After a refund

Your subscription ends. Because keys are checked offline, the key you already have keeps working until its expiry date, then ro goes back to the free version.
