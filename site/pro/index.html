Why you can trust it

It doesn't touch your files.

Search only reads. Tidying is optional and moves only what you approve. Nothing is deleted for good unless you confirm it yourself in Terminal.

Your files never leave your Mac.

Search doesn't use an AI service and doesn't send file contents or lists of paths. The only other thing ro does online is confirm your purchase and renewals, sending a random install ID and nothing else. You can turn that off.

Secret files stay out of results.

Password and key files are excluded from search.

What isn't proven yet

On automatically generated test questions, the right file was in the top 3 results 86–90% of the time, and in the top 5 90–92% of the time (measured 2026-10-30). We are still measuring with real questions and will publish the result here.

Questions

How many Macs can I use it on?

All the Macs you personally use. Please don't share pro with other people.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Pro features run on your Mac. ro goes online only to check for app updates and to confirm your purchase and renewals.

What happens if I cancel?

Pro keeps working until the end of the period you paid for, plus 14 days. Then ro goes back to the free version. Your files and history stay as they are.

pro didn't turn on after I paid

Keep ro open and online for a minute. Still free? Manage or ask about your order in Polar's customer portal or write to our support address (coming soon).

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 14 days. See the refund policy.

Does it run on Intel Macs or Windows?

No. ro needs an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 13 or later.
