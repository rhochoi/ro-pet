Why you can trust it

  • No unapproved writes. Search only reads. Tidying moves only what you approve and never deletes files without your Terminal confirmation.
  • Local-first. Search never sends file contents or paths anywhere. Online requests are limited to app updates and picking up your pro key via a random install ID (which you can turn off).
  • Secrets excluded. Password and key files stay out of search results.

Questions

How many Macs can I use it on?

All the Macs you personally use. Please don't share your key with other people.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once pro is on. The key is checked on your Mac, and pro features run on your Mac. ro goes online to check for app updates and, if you bought from ro, to pick up your key and renewals.

What happens if I cancel?

Pro keeps working until your key expires: the end of the period you paid for, plus 14 days. Then ro goes back to the free version. Your files and history stay as they are.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 14 days. See the refund policy.

Does it run on Intel Macs or Windows?

No. ro needs an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 13 or later.
