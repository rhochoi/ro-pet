What pro does

Find files by asking

By kind and time ("the contract PDF I got last month"), by a half-remembered name or a typo, and by the words inside documents and scanned images.

⌥R → /file

Tidy your Downloads

ro suggests where each file goes. It moves only what you approve, puts unneeded files in a holding folder instead of deleting them, and undoes everything in one step.

Organize tab

Switch AI tools without re-explaining

Out of tokens in one AI tool? ro writes a handoff note from the history that tool left on this Mac, hides secrets, and opens the next tool. You paste it. ro never sends it anywhere.

/handoff

Connect your AI tools safely

See which AI tools share your rules and safety hook. ro shows the exact changes first and writes only the ones you approve.

/connect

Why you can trust it

  • It doesn't touch your files. Search only reads. Tidying is optional and moves only what you approve. Nothing is deleted for good unless you confirm it yourself in Terminal.
  • Your files never leave your Mac. Search doesn't use an AI service and doesn't send file contents or lists of paths. The only other thing ro does online is pick up your pro key, sending a random install ID and nothing else. You can turn that off.
  • Secret files stay out of results. Password and key files are excluded from search.

What isn't proven yet

On automatically generated test questions, the right file was in the top 3 results 86–90% of the time, and in the top 5 90–92% of the time (measured 2026-10-30). We are still measuring with real questions and will publish the result here.

Questions

How many Macs can I use it on?

All the Macs you personally use. Please don't share your key with other people.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once pro is on. The key is checked on your Mac, and pro features run on your Mac. ro goes online to check for app updates and, if you bought from ro, to pick up your key and renewals.

What happens if I cancel?

Pro keeps working until your key expires: the end of the period you paid for, plus 14 days. Then ro goes back to the free version. Your files and history stay as they are.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 14 days. See the refund policy.

Does it run on Intel Macs or Windows?

No. ro needs an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 13 or later.
